# Freelance Projects

Portfolio case study source notes for three independently built client applications. Live URLs and implementation details were supplied by the developer or reviewed from the local project source. Where dates, business results, or client feedback are not evidenced, they are explicitly left unclaimed.

## Saanvi Services

- **Summary:** Website for Saanvi Services, presenting personalised disability support and home services.
- **Live website:** [saanviservices.com.au](https://www.saanviservices.com.au/)
- **Purpose and audience:** Helps people with disabilities, their families, and other prospective clients understand and enquire about individual disability support, cleaning, and yard maintenance services.
- **Role:** Solo product designer and developer. End-to-end design and implementation; deployment details and project dates to confirm.
- **Key pages and features:** Home, About, Services, Team, and Contact pages; service descriptions, enquiry/contact pathways, and team information.
- **Technology:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, React Router, Framer Motion, React Hook Form, Zod, and EmailJS. Hosting/provider not established from the local source.
- **Design:** Warm, people-centred presentation with responsive layouts, service-focused content, and subtle motion.
- **Results:** No verified analytics, conversion, performance, or client feedback figures available in the project materials.
- **Images:** Desktop homepage screenshot is available at `public/projects/saanvi-services-desktop.png`. A mobile screenshot can be added at `public/projects/saanvi-services-mobile.png` when available.

## Tiffin Home & Restaurant

- **Summary:** Website and booking experience for a family-run lodge and restaurant in Sauraha, Chitwan, Nepal.
- **Live website:** [tiffinhomeandrestaurant.netlify.app](https://tiffinhomeandrestaurant.netlify.app/)
- **Purpose and audience:** Helps travellers explore accommodation, restaurant offerings, tours, and local information, and submit booking or general enquiries.
- **Role:** Solo product designer and developer. End-to-end design and implementation; deployment details and project dates to confirm.
- **Key pages and features:** Home, rooms and room details, restaurant, tours, story, location, FAQ, and gallery; room booking and availability flow; enquiries; booking confirmation; admin pages.
- **Technology:** Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, MongoDB/Mongoose, NextAuth, Zod, Nodemailer, and OpenNext/Cloudflare deployment configuration. Runtime database and email services require environment configuration.
- **Design:** Green brand palette, responsive layouts, property and activity imagery, date-based room booking, and reduced-motion support.
- **Results:** No verified analytics, booking conversion, performance, or client feedback figures available. The site/project content describes a family-run business with 10 rooms and more than 20 years in Sauraha; these are business details presented by the project, not measured outcomes.
- **Images:** Desktop homepage screenshot is available at `public/projects/tiffin-home-desktop.png`. A mobile screenshot can be added at `public/projects/tiffin-home-mobile.png` when available.

## G.S. Motors

- **Summary:** Digital showroom for browsing second-hand vehicles and requesting a quote to sell a car.
- **Live website:** [gsmotors.com.np](https://www.gsmotors.com.np/)
- **Purpose and audience:** Connects car buyers and sellers with a Kathmandu dealership through searchable vehicle listings, detailed car pages, and seller quote requests.
- **Role:** Solo product designer and developer. End-to-end design and implementation; project dates to confirm.
- **Key pages and features:** Home, listings with filters, car details, gallery, About, sell-your-car form with image uploads, and protected admin tools for managing cars and quote requests.
- **Technology:** React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Express, MongoDB/Mongoose, Zod, and Vercel deployment configuration; image storage integration is present in the backend.
- **Design:** Forest green and cream visual identity, responsive layouts, animated hero and odometer counters, listing filters, and reduced-motion support.
- **Results:** No verified analytics, leads, conversion, performance, or client feedback figures available. Business claims displayed by the site should not be presented as project results without client verification.
- **Images:** Desktop homepage screenshot is available at `public/projects/gs-motors-desktop.png`. A mobile screenshot can be added at `public/projects/gs-motors-mobile.png` when available.
